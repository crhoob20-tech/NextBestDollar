NEXT BEST DOLLAR — MONTHLY ALLOCATION

INSTALL: close the app, copy ALL .py files into your existing app folder, replace
matching code files, keep next_best_dollar.db unchanged, and run main.py.
This ZIP has all required modules and no database.

Plan now has Monthly allocation and Account projection tabs.
The allocation uses take-home income after payroll deductions, living expenses,
total debt payments including minimums, savings, Roth IRA and brokerage remainder.
Loan and savings examples start at $1,000 (loans at least recorded minimums).
Edit these targets. Your latest instructions supersede the workbook's $600 savings.
Gross salary is optional scenario context. Taxes and take-home pay are not inferred.
Use expected take-home pay to model a future full-time job. Save the allocation
scenario separately; it does not overwrite actual income, balances or expenses.

A Roth check or an explicit Skip Roth selection is required before the remainder
is invested. Verified rules cover 2026 only, using your supplied facts. The monthly
Roth figure spreads remaining room across 1–12 chosen months, rounded down. Future
jobs/tax years require new eligibility checks; 2026 facts are only an illustration.
The allocation is a monthly snapshot, not a multi-year tax or debt-payoff schedule.
Loan payments must cover recorded minimums. If resources are insufficient, the
screen shows the shortfall and reduces later allocations. No negative remainder
is treated as investable money. Saved scenarios can be underfunded and are labeled.

Explore this contribution opens the corresponding account projection with that
funded monthly amount. It does not transfer money or create an account. Account
projections remain separate scenarios, not a combined multi-account simulation.
No bank integration, credentials, automatic imports or transfers are included.

Checked: priority order, cash conservation, minimum payment validation, income
shortfalls, zero brokerage remainder and invalid numeric inputs. All modules
compile. Please check the Mac interface after installation.

LIFE & DEBT TIMELINE
Plan > Life & debt timeline: review current cash flow, optionally choose the
graduation/new-job month and future take-home pay and living costs. Set debt
payments and savings; remaining dollars flow to taxable investing. Compare
starting money plus contributions against an editable compound-growth estimate.
Debt minimums come first, followed by highest-APR extra payments. Choose where
to redirect the former debt budget after all modeled debts are paid. Expand
yearly milestones for years 1-5 and each 5-year interval thereafter.
This separate scenario excludes retirement balances and payroll contributions;
use Account projection for IRA eligibility and retirement planning. A saved
timeline reloads inputs; current debt records are used when recalculating.
No bank connection or statement import is included.
