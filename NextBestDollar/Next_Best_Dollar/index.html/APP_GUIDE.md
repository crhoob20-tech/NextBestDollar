# NextBestDollar Guide

## Why NextBestDollar exists

NextBestDollar helps people connect everyday money decisions with the future they want. It starts with a real financial picture, helps users give each available dollar a purpose, and makes the tradeoffs visible.

The goal is not to tell someone what to do with their money. The goal is to make the next useful decision easier to understand.

## Getting started

Complete the three foundation sections:

1. **Personal facts** provides life context, such as age, work situation, household, and experience.
2. **Money habits** records planning preferences. There are no right answers.
3. **Financial facts** brings together take-home income, recurring expenses, cash, investments, debts, and benefits.

After saving Financial facts, download a private profile backup. The app keeps information in the current browser session, so the backup is how a user can restore progress later.

## What each area does

### Overview

Overview shows monthly breathing room, net financial position, emergency savings, goals, and spending insights. The budget insights compare planned monthly categories and support a monthly actual-spending check-in.

### Goals

Goals turn priorities such as emergency savings, debt payoff, a home, or travel into visible milestones. Users choose the target, progress, and timing.

### My allocation

Allocation divides the money left after planned living expenses and required debt payments among cash savings, a Roth IRA when the user has independently confirmed eligibility, brokerage investing, and extra debt payoff.

It also illustrates what that mix could do: an estimated debt-free timeline, time to a six-month emergency reserve, and possible brokerage growth by a chosen retirement age.

### Forecast

Forecast supports three separate planning views:

- **Focus on goals:** debt-payoff and saved-goal timelines.
- **Forecast an account:** compound-growth illustrations for investment accounts.
- **Explore a job or income change:** a private scenario showing how different take-home pay or costs could affect monthly cash flow.

## How calculations work

Debt illustrations use the saved balance, APR, and monthly payment. The allocation view applies extra debt money to the highest APR balance first. These estimates assume fixed interest rates, fixed payments, no new borrowing, and no fees.

Investment illustrations use a 10% long-term S&P 500 planning assumption by default. It is a historical planning reference, not a prediction. Markets can rise or fall, and investing can lose value.

## Data and privacy

NextBestDollar does not currently create accounts, connect to a bank, or keep a cloud database. Information remains in the current browser session. Downloaded JSON backups contain personal and financial details, so users should save them somewhere private.

## Beta feedback

Open **Beta feedback** from the app sidebar to rate the experience and describe what helped, confused, or felt missing. The app saves feedback on the tester's device. They can download a feedback-only file to share through the beta channel they were given. That file does not include their financial profile.

## Important limits

NextBestDollar is an educational planning tool. It does not provide tax, legal, investment, credit, or individualized financial advice. Users should verify account rules, contribution limits, eligibility, interest rates, and important decisions with the appropriate professional or provider.
